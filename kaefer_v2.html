📚 Bulk paste — historical weeks

Use this to backfill a year (or more) of weeks in one go.
Open your Excel hours register, select every row from the earliest week to the latest (including every WEEK XX and BREAK IN WORK separator between them), copy, and paste here. The tool detects each week automatically.
· The latest week becomes the current week.
· Every earlier week goes into the archive (searchable, viewable, exportable).
· Weeks with labels you already have archived are skipped to avoid duplicates.
